## What's Changed

### Video Frames

- Added `video frames` to export a first, last, numbered or timestamp-selected frame, a named frame set, or a whole-video sequence sampled by FPS or interval.
- Export PNG, JPG or WebP with format-specific quality choices, scaling and configurable filenames for frame sets and sequences.
- Added guided Interactive frame export with a coarse terminal timeline picker for quick selection, interval count estimates and generated filename previews.
- Added live frame-processing and write progress, with plain progress output when redirected and confirmed counts and destinations in the final result.
- Preserve supported source color samples during decoding and scaling, and embed ICC profiles in PNG, JPG and WebP. Support covers selected 8-bit RGB/YUV sources with BT.709 primaries and BT.709 or sRGB transfer. HDR, wider primaries, higher bit depths and reported source ICC profiles are rejected.
- Protect source media and unrelated destination files. Overwrite replaces only matching export images. Failure or cancellation retains completed images and reports partial results.
- Frames requires FFmpeg, FFprobe and the requested image encoder. Doctor now reports tool availability and advertised PNG, JPG and WebP encoder support. Source compatibility is checked during export.

### Reliability And Output

- Fixed valid PNG frame exports failing when their image data spans many chunks.
- Improved cancellation propagation in inline text/path editors and bounded streaming-tool shutdown, retaining encoder diagnostics on input failure.
- Frames and GIF now distinguish file and folder destinations and show concrete paths in export confirmation and results.

### Compatibility

- Codex SDK baseline for `v0.2.0`: `0.160.0`.
- Node.js requirement remains `>=22.23.0`.
- Video frames native export verification covers macOS and bounded synthetic sources. Linux/Windows execution and viewer behavior remain unverified.
